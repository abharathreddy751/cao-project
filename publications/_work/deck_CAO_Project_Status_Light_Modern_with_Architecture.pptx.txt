--- slide 2
Problem Statement
01 • PROBLEM

02

The scheduling challenge
Tasks in one workflow can have very different execution costs. / A simple scheduler may ignore task parameters that strongly affect runtime. / Choosing a worker by availability alone may miss a faster task–worker assignment. / The challenge is to reduce total workflow time while respecting task dependencies and worker availability.

Project question
Can task-cost estimates help the scheduler make better offloading decisions?

Research focus
Compare a parameter-aware scheduling policy with blind and informed baselines, then test whether the approach holds under different workloads.
Target outcome: measured improvement—not an assumed speedup.

What the metrics mean
Runtime change vs B4: negative means faster. CI: uncertainty range. Adjusted p-values support the hidden-cost comparison, not broad generalisation.
Key interpretation: parameter-aware scheduling helps on the tested hidden-cost case, but it is not better on every workload.
