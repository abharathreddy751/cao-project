LIVE Runs one block of the final benchmark on this laptop now: the real WorkerPool + Coordinator with P cores, E cores and (if chosen) the RTX 3050 CUDA worker G, the final workload (16 heavy chains of 64, 512 px) and the policy you pick. A demonstration run, not an experiment record: one repeat, no warm-up, so its makespan is not comparable with the confirmatory numbers on tab 3.

Takes about one to two minutes: worker start-up (and CUDA start-up for the GPU options), one run, then output verification (GPU tasks are re-run on the CPU and compared). Needs 2.4 GB of free RAM; close other programs first. Dynamic = decides while running; static = a fixed plan made before the run.
